## Pitch Lens — Hiptron / OneAIM Context

He's a startup-experienced, regulated-industry AI architect being shown a **non-medical mobility-insights retrofit** for rollators, judged on privacy, trust, emotional acceptance *equally* with technical accuracy. He'll instinctively map your pitch onto his Discovery→Crawl→Walk→Run lens.

### What he will focus on first
1. **The "people" layer**: who actually has to accept the device — the older adult, the adult child, the GP, the insurer? He'll ask which one of them is the buyer and which one is the user. He has seen 15% adoption become 82% adoption only with town halls + champions, and he'll want to know your equivalent.
2. **Knowledge architecture**: structured (gait/route models, baselines per user) vs. stochastic (LLM-generated explanations). He'll want a clean line between "this is what we measured" and "this is what the LLM said about it."
3. **Non-medical positioning, defensibly held**: he ships into Helsana/LGT, he knows where MDR/IVDR begins. He'll prod the boundary.
4. **Data governance & retrofit reality**: BLE + GSM + GPS streaming from someone's grandmother's flat. Where does the data live, who can access it, how do you withdraw consent, what's the EU AI Act classification?
5. **Adoption path & unit economics**: the retrofit angle interests him — he loves "minimize lock-in." He'll ask: who pays per unit, what's the per-user/month cost, what's the **time-to-first-meaningful-insight** for a family.

### Likely questions (in his voice)
1. *"You said non-medical. Walk me through the exact moment a behaviour change in your dashboard becomes a clinical signal — and what you do at that boundary."*
2. *"Who is the buyer of this thing? The Senior? The daughter? The pflegekasse? Your slides say all three. Pick one for the next 12 months."*
3. *"What's the difference between what your model **measures** and what your app **explains**? Are those the same component or different ones?"*
4. *"Show me your data flow on one slide — sensor to cloud to app, with retention, consent revocation, and the on-device vs. cloud split."*
5. *"You mention AI to detect meaningful change. Meaningful to whom? How do you avoid alert fatigue for the family and 'creep' fatigue for the user?"*
6. *"You're a retrofit. What happens when the rollator is sold, given to a neighbour, or replaced? Identity, re-pairing, baseline reset?"*
7. *"Crawl–walk–run: what does **crawl** look like for you in the next 8 weeks? One care home, one family, one feature — pick."*
8. *"Where's the human in the loop? Who reads the 'something changed' signal and decides nothing's wrong vs. someone should call?"*

### Likely objections (3–5)
1. **Non-medical hedge is structurally fragile.** "The minute a family member acts on your signal, you're functionally clinical, even if your label says otherwise."
2. **Sensor stack is a commodity; the moat is the behaviour model + the trust UX.** He'll worry you've over-indexed on hardware.
3. **Privacy/emotional acceptance is talked about, not architected.** He'll want to see specific controls, not a values statement.
4. **B2C/B2B2C ambiguity** — selling to seniors *and* care homes *and* insurers from day one is a classic founder trap he's seen rot pilots.
5. **35+ use cases tell him** that without a tight Discovery phase, this becomes a feature factory. He'll push back on scope.

### Hot buttons (things that make him lean in)
- Crisp ownership of the **people problem**: caregivers, family dynamics, dignity of the user.
- **Human-in-the-loop** and **hybrid** architecture, not "we trained an LLM on gait."
- A real **Discovery** plan with one named pilot partner and one named buyer.
- Honest discussion of **what you won't do** — what's out of scope.
- Open-source / no-lock-in posture for the cloud + model layer.
- Concrete **CHF/EUR per month per user** numbers, even rough.

### Red flags (things that make him cool off)
- "AI-powered" without specifying which knowledge type.
- Privacy treated as a compliance checklist instead of a design constraint.
- Demos that hide the failure mode (false positives → family panic).
- Hardware-heavy roadmap with no plan for the behaviour model.
- Founders who don't have a clear answer to "who pays?"
- Glossy slides without an execution path; he literally wrote that Europe risks irrelevance by mistaking vision for execution.

### His implicit scoring rubric [inferred from his frameworks]
- **People & adoption (30%)** — buyer/user clarity, change-management plan, dignity of the older adult
- **Architecture & data governance (25%)** — hybrid knowledge model, on-device vs. cloud, consent, retention, EU AI Act stance
- **Execution discipline (20%)** — Discovery → Crawl plan, one pilot, one feature, named partner
- **Trust & emotional acceptance (15%)** — explainability of "change detected," family UX, alert fatigue mitigation
